Character design · v3.4

Pepper

The robot children meet in class, dressed for a story. Pepper keeps its round white head, big eyes and chest screen, so children connect the drawing with the real robot standing beside them. The Piper costume adds the fairy-tale: a feathered cap, a pied tunic, a scalloped cape and a shawm pipe with a flared bell.

Signature pose: playing the pipe

Poses and when the game uses them

Each card is an animated GIF: right-click it and choose "Save image", or drag it straight into PowerPoint. It keeps moving in the slideshow.

Every pose is cheerful or curious. Pepper is never sad, angry or crying, and there is no "wrong" face: a mistake gets the curious pose and an invitation to try again together.

Colour palette

Flat colours with one dark outline, like a picture book. The robot stays white; the costume carries the colour.

Design rules

Keep it Pepper

  • Pepper's own head outline: dome top, ears bulging out at the sides, softer chin
  • Small, simple smile
  • No eyebrows at rest (the real robot has none); they appear only when thinking or curious
  • Big round eyes with a blue ring
  • Chest screen always visible (arms and pipe never cover it)
  • White bell-shaped base on wheels; no legs
  • Robot-white round hands

The Piper costume

  • Orange cap with a red band and a long pink feather
  • Pied tunic: purple left half, yellow right half
  • Red sleeves, so the arms read clearly against the tunic
  • Yellow scalloped cape with a white lining
  • Brown belt with pouch; curled red toes peeking out under the base
  • Shawm-style pipe: conical wooden body ending in a flared brass bell; played in the signature pose, tucked in the belt otherwise

Avoid

  • Sad, angry or crying expressions
  • Pure black outlines; use deep plum #2B2238
  • Covering the eyes or the screen with costume pieces
  • Rats or crowds of children from the original tale

Readability checks

SilhouetteCap, feather, pipe and cape make the shape recognisable even in solid black.
Small size (64 px)Eyes, cap and the purple/yellow split still read at the size used for icons.
On a dark groundThe plum outline keeps the white robot separate from any background.
